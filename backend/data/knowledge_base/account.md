# Account & Rewards

## Resetting your password
Click "Sign in" and then "Forgot password". Enter your email address and we send a reset link that is valid for 30 minutes. If the email does not arrive, check your spam folder.

## Updating account details
You can update your name, email address, saved addresses and payment methods under Account > Settings. Changing your email requires confirming the new address.

## Deleting your account
You can request account deletion under Account > Settings > Privacy. We permanently delete your personal data within 30 days, except data we must keep for tax purposes.

## Fernway Rewards program
Fernway Rewards is free to join. Members earn 1 point per dollar spent, and 100 points equals $5 in rewards. Members also get free standard shipping on every order, a 90-day return window and early access to sales.

## Newsletter and email preferences
You can unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link at the bottom of any email, or under Account > Settings > Notifications. Order and shipping emails cannot be turned off.
