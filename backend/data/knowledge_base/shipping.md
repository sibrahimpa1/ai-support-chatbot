# Shipping & Delivery

## Shipping options and delivery times
Fernway Outdoor ships from our warehouse in Denver, Colorado. Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days within the continental US. Express shipping takes 1-2 business days. Orders placed before 2 PM Mountain Time ship the same business day.

## Shipping costs and free shipping
Standard shipping is free on orders over $75. Below $75, standard shipping costs $6.95. Express shipping costs $19.95 on any order. Oversized items such as kayaks and canoes have a flat $49 freight fee.

## International shipping
We ship to Canada, the United Kingdom, the European Union and Australia. International delivery takes 7-14 business days. Customs duties and import taxes are paid by the customer and are not included in the order total.

## Tracking a shipment
As soon as your order ships you receive an email with a tracking number. You can also see tracking details under Account > Orders. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first scan after the label is created.

## Lost, late or damaged packages
If your tracking has not updated for 5 business days, or the package arrived damaged, contact support with your order number and photos of the damage. We will send a free replacement or issue a full refund. You do not need to return a damaged item.
