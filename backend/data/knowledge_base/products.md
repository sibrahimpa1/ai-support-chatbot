# Products, Sizing & Warranty

## Fernway lifetime warranty
All Fernway-brand gear is covered by a lifetime warranty against defects in materials and workmanship. Normal wear and tear, accidents and damage from misuse are not covered. To make a warranty claim, contact support with photos of the issue and your order number.

## Repairs
We repair Fernway tents, jackets and backpacks at our Denver repair center. Repairs for damage outside the warranty start at $15. Typical turnaround is 2-3 weeks.

## Sizing help
Every product page has a size chart. Fernway apparel runs true to size; if you are between sizes and plan to layer, choose the larger size. Hiking boots run half a size small, so we recommend ordering half a size up.

## Tent care
Always dry your tent completely before storing it to prevent mildew. Clean it with cold water and a mild soap, never in a washing machine. Re-apply a waterproofing spray once per season.

## Product availability and restocks
If an item is out of stock, click "Notify me" on the product page and we email you when it is back. Most popular items are restocked within 2-4 weeks.
