# About Fernway & Contact

## Support hours and contact options
Our human support team is available Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 6 PM Mountain Time, and Saturday 9 AM to 1 PM. You can reach us by chat, by email at help@fernway.example or by phone at (555) 014-2200. The AI assistant is available 24/7.

## Store location
Our flagship store is at 1450 Larimer Street, Denver, Colorado. It is open daily from 10 AM to 7 PM. You can pick up online orders in store for free, usually within 2 hours.

## About Fernway Outdoor
Fernway Outdoor is an online outdoor gear retailer founded in 2016. We sell tents, backpacks, apparel, footwear and paddling gear, and we donate 1% of sales to trail conservation.
