# Payments & Billing

## Accepted payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay and Fernway gift cards. You can also pay in 4 interest-free installments with Klarna on orders over $50.

## When you are charged
Your card is authorized when you place the order and charged when the order ships. If an order ships in multiple packages, you may see one charge per package.

## Promo codes and discounts
Enter promo codes in the cart before checkout. Only one promo code can be used per order, and codes cannot be applied to gift cards or to orders that were already placed. If a code is not working, check its expiry date and minimum order amount.

## Price match and price adjustments
If an item you bought drops in price within 14 days of purchase, we refund the difference. Contact support with your order number. We also match the price of identical items from major US outdoor retailers.

## Double charges and billing errors
If you were charged twice, the second charge is usually a temporary authorization (a pending hold) that disappears within 3-5 business days. If the charge does not disappear, contact support and a billing specialist will investigate.

## Gift cards
Fernway gift cards never expire and can be used online and in our Denver flagship store. You can check your gift card balance at checkout.
